I'm a Computer Science and Engineering graduate specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning from Sri Venkateswara College of Engineering, class of 2024. My work sits at the intersection of engineering and craft — I enjoy the logic of building systems that work reliably as much as I enjoy the design decisions that make them feel right to use.
